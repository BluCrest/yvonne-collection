## TECHNICAL NOTES FOR DEVELOPER

### Recommended Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 4
- **Animations:** Framer Motion
- **CMS:** Sanity (free tier — mom can update products)
- **Hosting:** Vercel (free tier)
- **Images:** Cloudinary (free tier — auto-optimization)
- **Payments:** Paystack (Nigerian payment gateway)
- **Analytics:** Vercel Analytics (free)

### Design Direction
- **Background:** White (#FFFFFF) primary
- **Accent:** Gold (#D4AF37) for CTAs, borders, highlights
- **Text:** Black (#000000) primary, dark gray (#333333) secondary
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (headings) + Inter or Lora (body)
- **Layout:** Minimal, lots of whitespace, large product images
- **Mobile-first:** 80%+ traffic will be mobile
- **Product cards:** Clean, white background, subtle hover animations
- **Hero:** Fullscreen image carousel with gold accent text

### Image Processing Notes
- Crop chin/face from all model photos (as requested)
- Use Cloudinary for auto-format (WebP/AVIF) and responsive sizing
- Product images: white background preferred, remove backgrounds where needed
- Hero images: lifestyle/editorial feel
